Data Story

Comparison of average summer temperatures in Boulder, Colorado from 2002 - present.

Boulder, Colorado is the home of University of Colorado Boulder with the infamous flatiron mountains. The summer months are a time for outdoor activities and relaxation. In Boulder, Colorado between the year 2002 and 2026, the average temperatures of June, July, and August have risen. In a world of rising concern for climate change, it is important to compare historical temperatures to recognize this change in order for these fun summer months to stay liveable.

D1 · Story Frame

This data story of rising summer temperatures in Boulder, Colorado is provided by resources from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), more specifically the NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory (PSL). Their mission is to "conduct weather, climate, and hydrologic research to advance the prediction of water availability and extremes".
Team, P. W. (n.d.). Boulder Monthly mean Temperature 1897-present: NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory. https://psl.noaa.gov/boulder/Boulder.mm.html
