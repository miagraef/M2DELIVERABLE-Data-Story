Data Story

Comparison of total snowfall in Colorado over the past 6 years.

The state of Colorado is known for their snowy winter seasons perfect for mountain adventures. However, over the past 6 years, the average snowfall has significantly declined each season. This decline in snowfall has resulted in many disappointed skiers. More importantly, it provides evidence for the global concern of climate change. It is crucial to compare snowfall data to recognize this change to educate the community and encourage climate action for future seasons.
